Dog on Chair Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

In short: Dog on Chair keeps your focus sessions, stats, collection and settings on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The only outside services involved are Google Play and RevenueCat, and only when you buy or restore Dog on Chair Pro.

Who we are

Dog on Chair is a focus timer app for Android, published by an independent developer. Questions about this policy: gcsamyog77@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following only in the app's storage on your phone. It is never uploaded to us:

  • Your focus sessions: when they ran, how long they were, and the label you gave them
  • Statistics and your session timeline
  • Collected items, room and dog choices, and the name you gave your dog
  • Settings such as break lengths, sound volume and motion preferences

Uninstalling the app, or choosing Settings → Reset all progress, deletes this information.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders, such as "focus done" and "break's over", locally on your phone. They are not sent through any server, and the app doesn't use push notifications.

Purchases (Dog on Chair Pro)

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not your name, email or Google account)
  • your purchase and subscription details from Google Play: product, purchase token, dates and status
  • basic technical details needed to process this: app version, device type, operating system version, country/currency and IP address

This is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and keep subscriptions working. See the RevenueCat privacy policy and the Google privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising and no advertising ID
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files
  • We don't sell or share personal information

Permissions

  • Notifications: to tell you when a session or break ends
  • Exact alarms: so that reminder arrives on time
  • Billing: to offer Dog on Chair Pro through Google Play

Children

Dog on Chair isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and your choices

Data on your phone stays until you reset progress or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat for as long as they need them for billing and legal reasons. To ask for your anonymous RevenueCat record to be deleted, email us. Include your Google Play order ID so we can find it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature uses data differently, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
